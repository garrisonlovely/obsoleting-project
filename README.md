# The Obsoleting Project

Companion site to *Obsolete: The AI Industry's Trillion-Dollar Race to Replace Us—and How to Stop It* by Garrison Lovely (OR Books & Nation Books, September 29, 2026).

The leading AI labs, presented deadpan as the single corporate consortium they would be if their shared objective had one website: mission, members, leadership, outlook, a 10-K-style risk factors excerpt, and careers. Every statement the Project "makes" is a real, on-the-record quotation or figure, footnoted in the Notes section. The page drops the act once, in the editorial note at the bottom, which points to the book.

- `index.html`: the site (single file, no build step; Archivo from Google Fonts)
- `old/`: the first version of the site, preserved at obsoletingproject.com/old/ (noindex)
- `og-image.png`, `favicon.svg`, `apple-touch-icon.png`: share card and icons

Deployed at: [obsoletingproject.com](https://obsoletingproject.com)
